# Lab 13 Grading Rubric — Advanced Network Configuration

This rubric applies to **both Option 1 (Conceptual)** and **Option 2 (Hands-On)**. You are graded on understanding, reasoning, evidence, and professionalism — not just correctness.

| Criterion | Excellent | Good | Needs Improvement | Points |
|-----------|-----------|------|-------------------|--------|
| **1. Conceptual Understanding & Accuracy** | Demonstrates strong, accurate understanding of advanced networking concepts (configuration persistence, routing behavior, DNS/name resolution). Explanations are technically correct and clearly reasoned. | Shows general understanding with minor inaccuracies or omissions. Core ideas are correct, but explanations may lack depth or precision. | Misunderstands or inaccurately explains key concepts. Responses show confusion between networking fundamentals and system behavior. | **15** |
| **2. Analysis, Reasoning & Troubleshooting Logic** | Applies a logical, structured troubleshooting approach. Clearly distinguishes between connectivity, routing, and name resolution issues. | Reasoning is mostly sound but may skip steps or rely on assumptions. Troubleshooting logic is present but not fully developed. | Lacks clear troubleshooting flow. Steps are missing, out of order, or unsupported by reasoning. | **10** |
| **3. Evidence & Application** | Evidence or explanations clearly support conclusions. Outputs/screenshots (if applicable) are relevant, readable, and correctly interpreted. | Evidence is present but may be incomplete, loosely connected, or only partially explained. | Little or no usable evidence provided, or evidence does not support conclusions. | **10** |
| **4. Clarity, Organization & Professionalism** | Responses are clear, well-organized, concise, and professional. Instructions are followed exactly. | Generally clear, but may include minor organization or clarity issues. | Disorganized, difficult to follow, or incomplete. | **5** |
| **5. Submission & Timeliness** | On time (submitted by the posted deadline). | 1 day late (within 24 hours after the deadline). | 2+ days late (within 48 hours = 1 pt; beyond 48 hours = 0 pts). | **5** |

**Total Possible Points: 40**
